# Fame Checker

The finale of this walkthrough describes the one lonely item in your pack that probably won't get too much use, the Fame Checker. This little gizmo will highlight information on characters you have talked to throughout the journey. Here's a list of all the characters and the places you obtain their six tidbits of information.

Note:Once you have all six bits of info, push start when highlighting that person to read a special bit of info aimed towards you from the person!

## Professor Oak

1. Signboard outside Oak's laboratory
2. Talk to Oak's assistant in Oak's laboratory
3. Talk to Oak in Oak's laboratory
4. Check the bookshelf in the Pokemon School House
5. Talk to Agatha in Indigo Plateau
6. Talk to Oak's Assitant after beating Elite 4
